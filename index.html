1. Introduction

Dynamic Bits ("we", "our", or "us") operates the casual mobile game Tangled Tails: Snake Puzzle. We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our game treats information and details our compliance with mobile privacy regulations and the Google Play Developer Policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location to play the game.

3. Local Device Storage

The game saves your progress locally on your Android device using standard local preferences:

  • Unlocked levels, stars, and high scores
  • In-game virtual coin balance
  • Unlocked creature skins and shelters
  • Audio, music, and vibration preferences

This data is stored purely on your local hardware and is never uploaded to any external server or third-party cloud database by us.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

To keep the game free for all players, Tangled Tails: Snake Puzzle displays advertisements using Google AdMob (Google Play Services).

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous technical information to serve relevant or non-personalized advertisements, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (GAID)
  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and screen resolution
  • General network connection status and approximate (country/city level) IP location
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics

All ad data processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. For more details on how Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services, please review: Google Advertising Privacy & Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

5. In-App Purchases (IAP)

Tangled Tails: Snake Puzzle does NOT contain any real-money In-App Purchases (IAP). All virtual currency (coins) and items can only be earned through gameplay achievements, daily rewards, or optional rewarded ads. We do not process, collect, or store any financial, credit card, or payment data.

6. Permissions Requested

The game only requests standard Android permissions necessary for gameplay functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by Google AdMob to load ads.
  • VIBRATE: Required to provide tactile haptic feedback on arrow bumps and successful escapes (can be toggled off at any time in Game Settings).

7. Children's Privacy

Our game is a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided any personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to ensure it is addressed.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective Date".
